ET - Semi-structured interview

Start of the study:

Thanks for taking part in this student research project. The purpose of this interview is to understand what life is like as a Master’s student at Kingston University, including your studies, daily routine, social life, and any challenges you experience. There are no right or wrong answers. I’m interested in your personal experience, so please answer as openly as you feel comfortable. Do you have any questions before we begin?

Is it okay if I audio record this interview for research purposes?

​

Section 1 - Warm up questions

  1. Could you tell me a bit about yourself?
  2. What course are you studying?
  3. Do you have any work experience?
  4. Where are you from?

​

Section 2: Day-to-Day Student Life

  1. What does a typical day or week look like for you?
  2. How do you usually balance studying, assignments, and your personal life?
  3. What do you enjoy most about being a student at Kingston?
  4. What has been the most challenging part?

​

Section 3: University & Social Life

  1. How would you describe your experience with other students?
  2. Do you feel like you’ve been able to make friends or meet people?
  3. How do you usually spend your time outside of classes?
  4. Is there anything about university life that you wish was easier?

​

Section 4: Looking Back

  1. If you could give advice to someone starting a Master’s at Kingston, what would you tell them?
  2. What is one thing you wish you had known before starting?
  3. Overall, how would you describe your Master’s experience so far?

​

Closing

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. Is there anything else about your experience as a Master’s student at Kingston that you think would be useful for me to know?
